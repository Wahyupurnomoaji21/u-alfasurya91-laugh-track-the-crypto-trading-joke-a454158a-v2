## About

This is the v2 entry for the make.fun competition "Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest": one line, under 200 characters, about crypto and trading life. An AI judge compares entries two at a time, weighing the laugh, the truth of trading life, and the economy of the line.

The v1 entry ("Still Early") ranked 1086/1998 in round 2, outside the top 1000. This version tries a different, more visual gag: "buy the dip" is the one piece of trading advice every crypto trader has heard and followed. The setup repeats the dip three times, each one lower than the last, so the fall keeps accelerating. The payoff reframes the wrecked portfolio as a support group — the coins are not investments any more, they are patients, and recovery is never coming. Punchline at the end, every word earns its place.
